# What the system will not claim

This page is mirrored in the app at `/limits`.

The system will **not**:

1. Claim plant and animal proteins are equivalent.
2. Equate non-heme iron, phytate-bound zinc, or carotenoid-A with heme iron, animal zinc, or retinol.
3. Treat ALA as EPA/DHA.
4. Count algal B12 analogues as vitamin B12.
5. Call a plant-only diet complete without fortification or supplementation.
6. Moralise fibre absence in animal foods or fibre presence in plants as overall superiority.
7. Collapse leafy salads, legumes, sprouts, kraut, mushrooms, and algae.
8. Collapse muscle, organs, eggs, dairy, and fermented animal foods.
9. Diagnose, prescribe, or interpret personal bloodwork (future overlay only).
10. Present residue scores as farm-level lab certificates.
11. Hide agricultural chemicals, metals, or veterinary residues.
12. Emit a black-box AI nutrition score.
13. Put access behind a paywall.

It **will** rank within class, show limiting amino acids and coefficients, version the data, and export the matrix.
