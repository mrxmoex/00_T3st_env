# D1 handoff: fruit, nuts and seeds, grains

## Intent

Grow the catalog where it was thinnest (E3: produce and ingredients, important subject areas). Before D1 the app had no fruit, no nuts or seeds, and no grains. D1 adds three plant classes and 42 foods, from 96 to 138, with every nutrient imported by script from BLS 4.0 and USDA SR Legacy.

## What was added

| Class | Foods |
|---|---|
| Fruit (`fruits`, new) | apple, pear, banana, orange, kiwi, strawberries, blueberries, raspberries, blackcurrants, grapes, avocado, apricot (raw and dried) |
| Nuts & seeds (`nuts_seeds`, new) | almonds, walnuts, hazelnuts, cashews, peanuts (roasted), pistachios, Brazil nuts, sunflower, pumpkin, flax, chia, sesame |
| Grains & pseudocereals (`grains`, new) | oat flakes and porridge, wholemeal rye bread, wholemeal and white wheat bread, brown and white rice, refined and wholemeal pasta, quinoa |
| Other vegetables | onion, garlic, leek, cucumber, asparagus, green beans |
| Legumes | green peas (frozen, boiled) |

Buckwheat and millet were left out: BLS lists them only raw or as recipes with added fat and salt.

## Truth ledger

**Imported values** (`src/data/sources/manifest.json`, `snapshot.json`)
- BLS is primary for 40 foods; USDA fills what BLS does not report (choline, selenium, missing amino acids).
- USDA is primary for pistachios and pumpkin seeds, and the manifest says why: BLS lists 5.6 mg potassium for pistachios and folate of exactly 0 (formula) for pumpkin seeds (`docs/data-sources.md` rule 7).
- Raspberries and blackcurrants have no amino acids in either database; the strawberry pattern fills them, labelled `pattern` (BLS *Musterberechnung*). Their notes say so.
- Every new BLS entry was compared with its USDA match. Differences of three times or more were kept when explainable: US flour fortification (white bread calcium, iron, folate), fibre method (garlic: BLS 28 g fibre, 3 g available carbohydrate; USDA 2 g and 31 g; the garlic note states both), natural variation (almond calcium, leek magnesium). Only the two impossible values above changed the source.

**Curated values** (`src/data/foods/fruits.ts`, `nutsSeeds.ts`, `grains.ts`, `vegetables.ts`)
- Protein digestibility, with its basis on each food (`src/data/foods/proteinDigestibility.ts`, shown in the Food page source panel):
  - **Cited:** oat flakes 0.86, wholemeal wheat bread 0.86, polished rice 0.88, peanuts 0.95 (FAO/WHO/UNU 1985, Table 36, human, faecal); white bread 0.90 (Walther et al. 2019, human ileal, wheat toast).
  - **Proxies, labelled as such:** rye bread 0.86 (whole wheat), pasta 0.90 (wheat toast), wholemeal pasta 0.86 (whole wheat), green peas 0.89 (pea flour), brown rice and quinoa 0.85 (FAO 1985 whole-grain cereal value).
  - **Estimates, labelled as such:** other nuts and seeds 0.85; fruit 0.75 and the new vegetables 0.74–0.76, set with the existing vegetable values.
- Phytate flag (`zincBoundByPhytate`): whole grains, nuts, seeds, green peas yes; fruit, alliums, refined grains no.
- Resistant starch: 0 for every new food, so they do not deepen the open double-count question.
- Residue and stability profiles: class templates on the existing scale (berries high surface, peeled fruit low, kernels low; cadmium-accumulating oilseeds "moderate" metals; rice "moderate" metals). No per-commodity EFSA rates yet; see *Found, not fixed*.
- Phytochemical index: judged within class, like the existing catalog; no per-value citation (see *Found, not fixed*).
- Notes: each states only what the imported values show (for example, brown rice has about three times the magnesium of white rice: 43 mg against 14 mg).

**Class design**
- Weights sum to 1 (test): fruit leans on micros, fibre, carbohydrate, and residue; nuts and seeds on fat quality and micros; grains on carbohydrate quality and fibre.
- Fibre baselines: fruit 0.75, nuts and seeds 0.65, grains 0.45 (`docs/scoring-formulas.md`).
- Class columns: fibre per sugar, potassium density, ALA density, vitamin E density, magnesium density, thiamin density, plus existing lysine adequacy and phytate (full-scale values in `docs/scoring-formulas.md`).
- Taurine: "not detected" (Laidlaw et al., 48 plant foods) applies to fruit, nuts, seeds, and vegetables; grains show "no data", because that analysis covered no cereals.

**Pipeline**
- Rule 6 (starch from the same entry as available carbohydrate) came out of this work; see the Phase 3 handoff for the nine values it corrected.
- `scripts/data/find_food.py` searches the cached archives by name and flags missing amino acids.
- The naming test now accepts BLS's bare names for unroasted nuts and seeds ("Walnuss") and "-brot" for baked bread, and still rejects an entry that names a different preparation.

## Decisions

- **Three classes, not one "plant staples" bucket**: non-claim 7 now lists fruit, nuts and seeds, and grains among the classes the app never collapses (doc and in-app).
- **No fats and oils yet**: an oil has no protein, and the EAA displays would show a digestibility of absent protein. That needs a "not applicable" display first (D2 prerequisite).
- **Proxies over silence, labelled**: a proxy names what it stands in for, so the source panel tells the truth about each number.

## Verified

- `npm test` (90), `npm run build`, `npm run lint`; the snapshot rebuilds byte-identical for the original 96 foods before D1.
- Phase 3 browser run covers the new classes in the picker, walnut and oat flakes on the radars, Brazil nut selenium ⚠, and the flaxseed Food page.

## Found, not fixed

- EFSA residue rates per commodity, contaminant exposure rules (inorganic arsenic in rice, aflatoxins in nuts, acrylamide in bread, cadmium in oilseeds), glycemic index values (Atkinson et al. 2021), and measured bioactives (coenzyme Q10, L-carnitine) for the new foods: a research pass is collecting cited values → D2.
- Phytochemical index has no per-value citation in the whole catalog; anchoring it to Phenol-Explorer totals → D2 or Phase 6.
- Almond and garlic calcium differ threefold or more between BLS and USDA; kept BLS as primary → note only.
- Resistant-starch double count across the older catalog → still needs the user's decision.

## Seeds for the next phase

D2: fats and oils (after the protein-free display), the research findings above, and more produce (berries' cooked forms, cabbage-family greens, fish and dairy staples).
