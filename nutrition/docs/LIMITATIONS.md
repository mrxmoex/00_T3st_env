# What we will NOT claim

This evaluation is **matrix-based biochemical context**, not medical advice, personalized nutrition, or a diet prescription.

## Explicit non-claims

1. **No "superfood" or moral ranking of diets** — scores describe biochemical profiles within stated axes, not human virtue or environmental ethics.
2. **No plant-only complete protein** without qualification — plant EAA completeness is scored honestly; we do not state that any single plant food replaces animal complete protein without fortification or deliberate pairing.
3. **No clinical outcomes** — we do not claim that tier S prevents or treats disease.
4. **No personalized requirements** — no age, pregnancy, athletic, or medical adjustments in v1.
5. **No real-time contamination data** — residue scores use published MRL *tier templates* per crop class, not your grocery batch's lab results.
6. **No cooking/recipe simulation** — degradation scores are sensitivity indices, not predictions for your kitchen technique.
7. **No supplement equivalence** — fortified nutrients in processed foods are flagged but not equated to whole-food matrices without context.
8. **No AI-generated nutrient values** — all numbers trace to listed sources or documented coefficients; missing data stays absent, not inferred.
9. **No paywall or "premium accuracy"** — the public dataset and formulas are the full model.
10. **No interchangeability of classes** — algae ≠ mushrooms ≠ legumes; separate divisions and weights apply.

## Dietary pattern filter behavior

- **Vegan / plant-only:** Surfaces explicit gaps (B12, EPA/DHA, heme iron, complete EAA) as informational flags, not hidden penalties.
- **Omnivore:** Shows full matrix without special casing.
- **Low residue:** Emphasizes axis 6; does not guarantee regulatory compliance.

## Regulatory

Not a food label, not FDA/EU-approved nutrition software. For education and research transparency.
